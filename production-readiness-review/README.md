# Bitcoin Production Readiness Review Framework

A practical framework for evaluating whether a Bitcoin product is ready for production.

## Purpose

Help engineering teams systematically identify security, reliability, operational, and scalability risks before launching or significantly expanding a Bitcoin product.

## Review Areas

- Architecture and trust boundaries
- Wallet and key management
- Transaction and payment flows
- Bitcoin and Lightning infrastructure
- Backend services and APIs
- Security controls
- Monitoring and observability
- Backup and recovery
- Operational readiness
- Scalability considerations

## Risk Classification

- **Critical** — Direct risk of fund loss or severe compromise
- **High** — Significant security or reliability risk
- **Medium** — Notable weakness
- **Low** — Improvement opportunity

## Outcome

A prioritized list of risks, recommended remediation steps, and clear next actions.
